# Inclusion Criteria

This resource includes studies of LLM-, VLM-, or multimodal foundation-model-driven medical AI-agent systems. A system must have a medical, health-care, public-health, biomedical, medical-education, administrative, or health-system workflow objective and report at least one agentic property, such as planning, tool use, retrieval, memory, iterative control, workflow interaction, simulation interaction, or multi-agent coordination.

Evidence provenance, boundary control, human reviewability, escalation rules, audit records, and lifecycle monitoring are charted as safety/governance variables rather than required inclusion criteria.
