# Inclusion criteria

Reports must describe an LLM-, VLM- or multimodal-foundation-model-based agent with an explicit medical or biomedical objective. The operational criteria require an identifiable current task state, a qualifying control decision made by the model, a linked downstream action, and distinguishable state-dependent trajectories. Agent terminology or retrieval/tool use alone is not sufficient.

The evaluated target must be a complete qualifying workflow with identifiable inputs, final outputs and task-level outcomes, executed in the stated evaluation environment. Isolated predictive models, static responses, component-only evaluations without a qualifying complete workflow, conceptual papers, reviews and protocols without empirical results are not included in the analytical denominator.

Cross-domain systems qualify only when the medical/biomedical configuration, procedure and results can be identified separately. Reports are the counting unit, not necessarily independent systems or investigations. Background citations outside the 54-report sample do not contribute to its frequencies. R255 is excluded.

Evidence provenance, boundaries, escalation, oversight and lifecycle controls are coded descriptively rather than required as proof of eligibility or safety. These rules describe the revised analysis; they are not presented as a prospectively registered protocol.
